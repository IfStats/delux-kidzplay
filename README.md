# Delux Kidzplay

**Bringing Your Playground to Life.**

Delux Kidzplay & Entertainment is a playground and children's recreational services company based in Accra, Ghana.

## About

Delux Kidzplay provides playground, recreational and children's entertainment solutions for schools, organizations, families and other clients.

### Core Services

- Playground Equipment Supply & Installation
- School Playground Design & Setup
- Kids Funfairs & Entertainment Events
- School-Based Recreational Programs

## Website

The Delux Kidzplay website is being developed as a modern, responsive digital platform showcasing the company's services, projects, gallery, testimonials and contact options.
